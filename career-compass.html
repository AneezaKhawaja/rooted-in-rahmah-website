About

A little about your coach

I'm Aneeza, a consultant at Deloitte, supporting the federal health sector with AI adoption and enablement work. Before that, I spent about three years at Accenture Federal Services. I hold dual master's degrees: one in Human-Computer Interaction from the University of Maryland and one in Systems Engineering from Johns Hopkins.

Career Compass grew out of a simple realization: the hardest part of a job search usually isn't the work itself, it's not knowing how the process actually works from the inside. I coach resume reviews, interview prep, and longer-term mentorship rooted in what I've learned navigating federal consulting, large-scale hiring processes, and my own career pivots.
