About

Why I bake

I'm Aneeza, and Sourdough Studio grew out of years of baking naturally leavened bread at home. Every loaf here is slow-fermented and baked in small batches, out of my own kitchen, with the same care whether it's your first loaf from me or your fiftieth.

I'm also always experimenting with what a sourdough starter can do beyond bread — I'd love to hear your ideas too, so if you've got a flavor or a use for starter you'd want to see me try, send it my way. I might just bring it to life.
