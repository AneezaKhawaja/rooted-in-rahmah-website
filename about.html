About Aneeza

Ellicott City, Maryland

I'm a federal AI enablement consultant by day, and someone who's always building something else on the side. Sourdough Studio and Career Compass both grew out of the same instinct — caring for people well, in more than one form.

I hold dual master's degrees in Human-Computer Interaction and Systems Engineering, and I've spent my career translating complicated technical and business problems into things people can actually use. That same instinct shows up differently in a loaf of bread or a resume rewrite, but it's the same care underneath.
